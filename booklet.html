We're holding a small ceremony at the breathtaking San Galgano Abbey in Chiusdino, Tuscany — a roofless 13th-century Gothic cathedral surrounded by rolling hills and cypress trees. Guests will spend the weekend at The Farmhouse at Terre di San Galgano, a 3-minute walk from the Abbey, where all festivities will be held.

Getting There

The closest airports are Florence (FLR) and Rome (FCO). We recommend booking flights once October 2026 schedules are available.

From either airport, take a train to Siena via Trenitalia — roughly 1.5 hours from Florence, or 3–4 hours from Rome, usually with one transfer. We suggest buying tickets on arrival in case of flight delays.

Need help with train times? Share your flight details via our travel form (forms.gle/oPDQTg5WWV8URcur9) and we'll gladly help coordinate.

Things to Do

A few of our favorites, right around the abbey.

Montesiepi Chapel & the Sword in the Stone

Just north of the abbey, a short walk up the hill, is Montesiepi Chapel — home to the famous "sword in the stone." Nearby you'll find a wine bar with local wine and snacks. Definitely worth checking out.

Trails & Hiking

There are several miles of trails around the grounds. A roughly 3-mile hike to the neighboring town of Monticiano starts behind the abbey — follow the trail down to the bridge, and hiking signs lead the rest of the way.
